Scope of this notice

Gdob is a mobile application focused on costume check-ins, makeup mapping, build progress logs, finished stage shots, con-related Inbox items, Backstage chats (including simulated voice or video sessions), and Badge profiles that reflect your circle and lookbook activity.

You can explore Gdob without a mandatory account system. The App does not require you to create a remote membership profile in order to open core screens. Where the App keeps preferences, posts, messages, or media, those records are described below based on how Gdob actually operates on the device.

This notice applies to information processed in connection with the iOS version of Gdob. It does not cover websites or services that we do not operate, even if you reach them from content shared inside the App.

Permissions the App may request

Gdob asks for system permissions only when a feature needs them. You can deny or later revoke access in your device settings. If you decline, related features may be limited while the rest of the App remains usable.

Photo library

Photo access supports avatars, Fit Check check-in images, Stage Shot media, Build Log reference photos, and images you attach in Backstage chats. Gdob reads the images you select; it does not need continuous scanning of your entire library to open the feed.

Camera

Camera access is used for optional Badge verification flows and for simulated video sessions in Backstage. The App does not claim to stream your camera to unrelated third-party advertising networks.

Microphone

Microphone access is used for simulated voice and video sessions in Backstage. If you refuse microphone access, call features that depend on audio will not work as intended.

Permission banners in Gdob explain these uses in plain language. Granting a permission is voluntary and can be changed at any time through the operating system.

Look, Inbox, Backstage, and Badge data

Depending on how you use Gdob, the App may process categories such as the following. We do not assert that Gdob collects nothing; the App stores and displays content you create and interactions you take.

Look station content

  • Fit Check entries: character or look names, optional spotlight tags (for example Wig, Makeup, Costume, Props), optional event labels, notes, and selected photos.
  • Makeup Map entries: titles, optional event links, zone pins on a face board, and short analysis notes.
  • Build Log entries: titles, materials, hours, progress milestones, optional photos, and notes.
  • Stage Shot entries: media you choose, optional titles, captions, and tag selections such as wig, makeup, armor, or stage.
  • Feed interactions: likes, dislikes, comments, saves, reports, and blocks that hide looks or authors from your view.

Inbox and events

Inbox surfaces con and shoot related items available in the App, including event names, timing cues, and related conversation threads. If you open those threads, message text and any media you send are processed to show the conversation.

Backstage communication

Backstage may include one-to-one or group chats, stickers you create or attach, group titles and member lists, and simulated call sessions that rely on camera or microphone permissions when you enable them.

Badge and circle context

Badge screens may show display names, circle affiliations, role tags, main character notes, cosplay-year context, lookbook lists (your looks, liked looks, saved looks), and optional avatar images. Age confirmation and optional face-related verification flags may appear in onboarding-style flows where the App presents them.

Device and diagnostic signals

Like most mobile apps, Gdob may receive ordinary technical signals from the operating system when features run (for example permission status, local file paths for images you save, and UI state needed to keep the feed responsive). We do not use this notice to invent separate analytics products that the App does not implement.

Storage on your device

Gdob persists application state locally so your looks, conversations, social graph choices (following, blocking, hidden posts), custom stickers, and activity lists can reload after you close the App. Local image files you import or capture for posts, avatars, or chat may be written to the App sandbox on your device.

Because this storage lives primarily on the device, clearing App data, uninstalling Gdob, or using system storage tools can remove local records. You should treat device backups according to your own backup settings; Gdob cannot guarantee that every backup environment excludes App files.

Network transmission and third parties

Features that simulate calls, load remote media assets bundled or referenced by the App, or communicate beyond the device may involve network connections. When network activity occurs, standard transport metadata (such as IP address and device connection details) may be processed by network providers and the operating system outside Gdob's direct control.

Gdob may rely on Apple platform services (photo picker, camera, microphone, tracking authorization prompts, and system UI) to deliver features. Those platform components process information under Apple's terms and privacy practices.

If you share screenshots or export content outside Gdob, the recipients of that content become independent controllers of whatever you send them. This notice does not govern their practices.

Personalization and tracking prompts

On supported iOS versions, Gdob may display Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The stated purpose in the App is to help personalize costume-related recommendations using profile, look, and interaction context. The prompt does not claim to process sports or match statistics.

You can allow or deny tracking through the system dialog and revisit the choice in device settings. Denying tracking may reduce personalization quality without removing your ability to browse looks or open Backstage.

Your choices and controls

  • Refuse or revoke photo, camera, microphone, or tracking permissions in system Settings.
  • Avoid attaching photos or notes you do not want stored with a look or chat message.
  • Use in-App tools such as report, hide, or block where available to limit what you see.
  • Delete looks you control where the App provides a delete action.
  • Remove the App from your device if you want local App storage cleared under ordinary iOS uninstall behavior.

If you need help locating a control or understanding what remains on your device after an action, write to proprail@gdobatelier.com.

Younger users

Gdob is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to consent to digital services under applicable laws in their place of residence. Where the App asks you to confirm age-related eligibility, that confirmation is part of keeping the community oriented toward responsible cosplay and costume-build sharing.

We do not knowingly design Gdob as a service directed to children who are younger than the age of digital consent. If you believe a minor has provided information in a way that should be reviewed, contact proprail@gdobatelier.com so we can assess appropriate steps under applicable requirements.

How long information stays

Look posts, comments, messages, stickers, Badge fields, and media files generally remain for as long as they are needed to provide the features you use, or until you delete them where deletion is available, or until local App data is cleared.

Cached or temporary files created by the operating system may persist according to system policies beyond Gdob's direct schedule. We do not promise a single global deletion timeline for every technical artifact on every device configuration.

Practical security measures

Gdob uses reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a mobile App that stores content locally and requests sensitive permissions such as camera and microphone. Measures may include sandbox storage, permission-gated access, and careful handling of media you explicitly select.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure. You should protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock and avoid sharing devices with people who should not see your looks or Backstage threads.

When this notice changes

We may revise this notice when Gdob features, permissions, or legal requirements change. The operative date shown in the information card at the top of this page will be updated when a revision takes effect. Continued use of Gdob after an update means the revised notice applies to subsequent processing described here.
